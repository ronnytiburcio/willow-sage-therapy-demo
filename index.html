Specialties

Support that meets you where you are

When your mind will not slow down

Racing thoughts, restless nights, the constant sense that something is about to go wrong. Together we untangle what fuels the worry and build practical ways to quiet it.

  • Evidence based approaches, explained in plain language
  • Tools you can actually use between sessions
  • Gentle pacing, nothing rushed

When giving everything leaves nothing for you

High achievers often arrive here running on empty. We look at the patterns that got you depleted and rebuild a sustainable relationship with work, rest, and expectations.

  • Untangle self worth from productivity
  • Set boundaries that hold up in real life
  • Recover energy without guilt

When life changes faster than you can process it

A move, a loss, a new role, an ending. Transitions shake our footing even when they are chosen. Therapy gives the change a place to land so you can move through it with intention.

  • Space to grieve what is ending
  • Clarity on what you want next
  • Steady support while things are in motion

When connection feels harder than it should

Recurring conflicts, distance that crept in, patterns you swore you would never repeat. We work on how you show up in relationships, starting with the one you have with yourself.

  • Understand your patterns without shame
  • Communicate needs before they become resentments
  • Individual work that improves every relationship

FAQ

Questions people ask before starting

What happens in the first session?
The first session is a conversation, not an interrogation. We talk about what brings you in, what you want to be different, and any questions you have about the process. You share only what you are ready to share.
How much do sessions cost?
Session rates are discussed during your free consultation, along with sliding scale availability. Superbills are provided so you can seek reimbursement from your insurance plan if it includes out of network benefits.
Do you offer telehealth?
Yes. Secure video sessions are available for clients who prefer meeting from home, including evening slots for busy schedules. Many clients mix office visits and video depending on the week.
Is everything I say confidential?
Yes, with the few legal exceptions every therapist follows, which we review together in plain language during your first session. Your privacy is treated with the utmost care.
How long does therapy take?
It depends on your goals. Some people come for a season around a specific challenge, others stay longer for deeper work. We check in regularly about progress so therapy serves you, not the calendar.
